YOUR FIRST CLIENT JOURNEY

Start with one venture.

Build a useful business workspace before expanding. You can test this journey now with sample information.

Preview works in this tab and resets on reload. Registration and private cloud storage await account-service activation. Agent execution and payments are not active.

Try the sample workspace

1. Define one offer

Choose a venture and write its purpose, customer, offer and next measurable milestone. Keep one commercial priority for the pilot.

2. Add your next actions

Add the six-week pilot plan. Edit the tasks to fit your service and mark completed work as you go.

3. Choose support

Browse the service catalogue, select useful services and carry the shortlist into your workspace. Shortlisting records interest; it activates no agents.

4. Record actual evidence

Record interviews, demonstrations and trials as projects. Capture what customers wanted, willingness to pay, work delivered and time or costs involved.

5. Review and decide

Use Reports to review saved progress and export your workspace. At six weeks, choose to continue, change the offer or pause based on evidence.
