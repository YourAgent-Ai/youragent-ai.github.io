Privacy information

This portal is prepared for private client accounts but its account backend is not yet configured. The preview holds sample records in tab memory and resets on reload; it does not create an account or upload document bytes.

When activated, the account provider handles email, passwords and authenticator factors. The database stores each account’s venture briefs, tasks, projects, requests, document metadata and activity. Documents use a private bucket. AI and third-party integrations are not activated.

Authenticated sessions use browser session storage. Page assets may be served by GitHub or another selected host; the connected portal loads its authentication library from jsDelivr. The operator must complete controller identity, provider and region details, retention periods, lawful basis, rights and a verified contact channel before accepting real client data.

This is launch preparation information, not a completed privacy notice.
