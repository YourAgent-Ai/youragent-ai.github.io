Frequently asked questions

Does every workflow need AI?

No. Tasks, project records and report calculations work without model calls.

Are agents running now?

No. The service catalogue and AI page describe potential capabilities.

Can I have several businesses?

Yes. Each venture has its own brief, tasks, records and document area.

Can I use my phone?

The interface adapts to mobile screens. A phone authenticator supplies the second factor after account setup.

Does recording an integration connect it?

No. It records a requirement for later implementation; no external credentials are collected.

Does a request send an email?

No. Requests are saved planning records. A business contact and operator workflow must be connected before correspondence works.
